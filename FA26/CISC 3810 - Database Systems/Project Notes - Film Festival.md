# Business Rules:
## 1.
- A CUSTOMER may purchase many TICKET

## 2.
- A SCREENING may have many TICKET

## 3.
- A FILM may be shown in many SCREENING
- Each SCREENING shows only one FILM
## 4.
- A VENUE may host many SCREENING
- Each SCREENING is hosted at only one VENUE
## 5.
- A DIRECTOR may direct many FILM
- Each FILM is directed by only one DIRECTOR
## 6.
- A FILM may belong to many GENRE
- A GENRE may include many FILM
## 7.

- An AWARD may have many FILM nominated
## 8.

- A FILM may be scored by many JUDGE
## 9.
- A SPONSOR may sponsor many SCREENING
- Each SCREENING may be sponsored by only one SPONSOR
## 10.
