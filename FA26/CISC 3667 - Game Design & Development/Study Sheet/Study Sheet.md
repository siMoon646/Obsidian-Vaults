# Confident:
**Game Design:**
- Creating rules, mechanics, experiences
**Iterative designing:**
- Repeatedly designing, testing, evaluating, and revising a game.
## LeBlanc's 8 Kinds Of Fun:
1) Sensation: Sensory Enjoyment
2) Fantasy: Suspend disbelief-- fun derived from imagination
3) Narrative: Fun derived from story
4) Challenge: Fun derived from achieving something difficult/overcoming a challenge.
5) Fellowship: Fun derived from community/socializing.
6) Discovery: Fun derived from exploring.
7) Expression: Fun derived from forms of self-expression and personalization.
8) Submission: Fun derived from surrendering mind to game.
## Lazzaro's Four Keys To Fun:
1) Hard Fun: Fun derived from doing something challenging.
2) Easy Fun: Curiosity, exploration, personalization.
3) Serious Fun: Meaning, personal value, and emotional impact.
4) People Fun:  Fun derived from interaction and shared experiences.
## Bartle's Four Player Types:
1) Achievers: Enjoy accomplishments, points, progress, and completion.
2) Explorers: Enjoy discovering game worlds and systems.
3) Socializers: Enjoy interacting and forming relationships with other players.
4) Killers: Enjoy competition, dominance, and acting on other players.
# Shaky:
**Lusory Attitude**:
 - Willingly accepting a game's rules and restrictions to play it.
## Four Elements Of Games:
1) Mechanics: Rules, procedures, and actions
2) Story: Events and narrative
3) Aesthetics: Game's sensory elements (picture/video/audio)
4) Technology: The medium(s) that make up the game.
## Objects, Attributes, and States:
- Object: 
	- an entity of a game
- Attribute: 
	- A property of an object
- State:
	- Current value of an attribute
## Actions:
- Basic Actions:
	- Actions directly allowed by the rules.
- Strategic Actions: 
	- The current value of an attribute.
## Five Types Of Rules:
1) Operational:
   - Instructions describing how to play.
2) Foundational:
   - Underlying mathematical or formal structure
3) Behavioral: 
   - Implicit expectations about appropriate behavior and sportsmanship. 
4) Written:
   - Rules documented for players.
5) House Rules:
   - Modifications made by players.
## Player Behaviors:
1) Standard Player:
	- Standard: Follows rules and plays intended.
2) Dedicated Player:
	- Follows the rules and while strongly pursuing mastery and victory.
3) Unsportsmanlike Player:
	- Follows formal rules but violates the spirit of play.
4) Cheat:
	- Breaks formal rules to gain an advantage.
5) Spoil-sport:
	- Rejects or disrupts the game itself.
## Strategies:
- Degenerate Strategies:
	- Consistently effective and undermines meaningful choices by making other strategies less viable.
- Exploit:
	- Using an unintended feature or interaction in a game's system to gain an advantage.
## Feedback Loops:
- **Positive Feedback Loop:**
	- Amplifies; snowball effect. Player is rewarded for doing well.
- **Negative Feedback Loop:**
	- Dulls; Player is resisted in reaching their goal. 
- **Dynamic Difficulty:**
	- Automatic adjustment of difficulty based on player performance.
## Randomness and Probability:
- **Randomness:**
	- Events with unpredictable outcomes.
- **Probability:**
	- The mathematical likelihood of an outcome.?
- **Theoretical Probability:**
	- Mathematical expected likelihood.?
- **Practical Probability:**
	- The frequency observed during actual trials
