1) Mechanics:
	- The procedures and rules of your game.
2)  Technology:
	- The objects, materials, and or medium through which the game is played.
3) story:
	- The narrative element behind the game.
4) Aesthetics:
	- The perception of the game--how it looks, sounds, feels.
5) Space \[game]:
	- The environment(s) that exist in the game, and how those spaces relate to each other.

# Action:
1) Strategic Action:
	- An action that the player takes to achieve something.
# Rules:
1) Operational Rules:
	- "What can the player do in the game"
2) Foundational Rules:
	- Formal rules underlying structure of the game, rules pertaining to mechanics of feedback from the game.
3) Behavioral Rules:
	- Implicit rules of behavior; "good sportsmanship"
4) written Rules:
	- The document that players read to understand the game
5) Laws:
	- Only formed when game are played in serious, competitive settings, where the stakes are high enough.
6) Official Rules:
	- Made when game is serious  enough that a group of players feels a need to merge the written rules with the laws.
7) Advisory rules: 
	- Tips to playing closer to most effective tactics available.
8) House rules:
	- When games are tuned to suit the tastes of a group of players, given that the participants consent to these amended rules.
