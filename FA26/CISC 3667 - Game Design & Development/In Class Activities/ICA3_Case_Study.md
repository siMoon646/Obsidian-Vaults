# 5) Why Did the Rules Change?

`> Wobbling being showed off in EVO 2013 as a strategy yielding second-place showed the competitive scene that it was a legitimate strategy. Banning wobbling from tournaments would leave players participating in those tournaments unprepared for the tournaments that DO allow wobbling. Years later, by 2019, the competitive scene had grown used to wobbling and gameplay became boring to watch as strategies concerned themselves too much with countering wobbling. They realized that wobbling was bad for the game's competitive entertainment and largely banned it going forward.`
